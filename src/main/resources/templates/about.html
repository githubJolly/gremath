About LetusLearn

A family tutor for the New Zealand Curriculum — lessons first, then practice, then a clear parent view.

LetusLearn is built for households who want more than a worksheet dump. Children open a year and subject, read a full lesson with worked examples, then sit an auto-marked practice sheet. Parents see the result immediately on the family dashboard and receive an email with the latest score plus averages by subject.

Teaching is original. We organise lessons to the year-by-year sequences on Tāhūrangi (English and Mathematics & Statistics Years 0–10, 2025, and the eight learning areas through Year 10). Official curriculum statements stay with the Ministry of Education.
