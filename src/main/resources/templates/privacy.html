Privacy

How LetusLearn handles family information.

What we store

Account username, child display name, login email, optional parent inbox, password hash, subscription and trial dates, practice scores, and locker rewards. We do not store card numbers, expiry dates or CVV values — Stripe handles checkout.

How we use it

To run the tutor, mark sheets, show the family dashboard, send verification and parent-progress emails, and process subscriptions you start. We do not sell student data.

Emails

Verification goes to the account email. Progress reports go to the parent inbox you set (or the account email if you leave that blank). You can turn progress emails off on the Progress page.

Retention and questions

You can ask us to close an account and delete stored practice history by writing to us from the email on the account. See Contact.
