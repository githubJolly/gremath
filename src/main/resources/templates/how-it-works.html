How LetusLearn works

Three steps for the child. A live subject report for the parent.

1

Choose year and subject

Years 1–10 and all eight NZ learning areas. Lessons match the year, not a one-size bank.

2

Learn, then sit the sheets

Goals, worked examples and common mistakes first. Then concept and word sheets with hints that never give the answer away.

3

Parents see the picture

After every sheet we email the parent inbox: latest score, average by subject, what needs another look, and what is going well.

What arrives in the parent email

  • The lesson just finished, sheet type, score and percentage.
  • A running average for Mathematics, English, Science, Social Sciences, Technology, The Arts, Health & PE, and Languages — whichever the child has practised.
  • Up to five focus areas below 70%, with a short next-step line.
  • A link back to the family dashboard.

You can change the parent inbox or pause emails from the Progress page after you log in.
